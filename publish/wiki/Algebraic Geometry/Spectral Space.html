    Section. spectral space [#]

      Definition. quasi-compact [#]

        • A topological space is quasi-compact if every open covering of has a finite subcover.
        • A continuous map is quasi-compact if the preimage of any quasi-compact open is quasi-compact.
        • A subset is retrocompact if the inclusion map is quasi-compact.

        Definition. quasi-separated [#]

          • A topological space is quasi-separated if the the diagonal is quasi-compact.
          • A topological space is quasi-separated if and only if intersection of any quasi-compact opens are still quasi-compact.
          • A continuous map is quasi-separated if the diagonal is quasi-compact.

                Definition. spectral [#]

                  • A topological space is called spectral if it is sober, quasi-compact, quasi-separated, and the collection of quasi-compact opens forms a basis for the topology.
                  • A continuous map is called spectral if the preimage of every quasi-compact open is quasi-compact.
